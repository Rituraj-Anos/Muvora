# DMCA & Copyright Policy

Muvora is a non-commercial, open-source music player. It **does not host,
store, upload, or distribute any copyrighted audio**. The application only:

- reads media already present on the user's own device, and
- streams media directly from public, public-facing, or user-authenticated
  third-party APIs (such as YouTube Music), at the direction of the user.

No music files are served from this repository, from any Muvora release, or
from the project website. Links to download content, song files, and streams
are provided by the third-party services themselves, not by Muvora.

## If you believe your rights are infringed

Because Muvora hosts no media, most copyright concerns are properly directed to
the platform actually hosting the content (for example, YouTube). If you believe
that material in **this repository or a Muvora release** (for example, source
code, artwork, or bundled assets) infringes your copyright, please send a notice
including:

1. Identification of the copyrighted work you claim is infringed.
2. Identification of the specific material in this repository or release that
   you claim is infringing, with a link or path so it can be located.
3. Your contact information (name, email address).
4. A statement that you have a good-faith belief the use is not authorized by
   the copyright owner, its agent, or the law.
5. A statement, under penalty of perjury, that the information in your notice is
   accurate and that you are the copyright owner or authorized to act on the
   owner's behalf.
6. Your physical or electronic signature.

Send the notice to the repository owner via a GitHub issue or the contact
listed on the owner's GitHub profile: <https://github.com/Rituraj-Anos>.

Valid notices concerning material actually hosted here will be acted on
promptly.
